WHAT CHANGES IN THE MARKETPLACE
- Filter approved offers by Product, Service, Wholesale or Rental.
- Filter by Fixed price, Starting price or Price on request.
- Sort by Newest, Oldest or offer name A–Z.
- Keep category, state and relevant search context when navigating marketplace category/state links.
- Show the number of matching offers returned, active filters, a clear-filter route and a free sourcing-request route when no published offer matches.
