# SEO and customer-acquisition playbook

## Search architecture
Build around demand, not around a fixed list of states. Each landing page should answer one clear commercial intent:
- `buy [product] in [location]`
- `[product] suppliers in [location]`
- `[product] wholesale price in [location]`
- `[service] companies in [location]`
- `[equipment] repair / maintenance in [location]`
- `request quote for [product/service] in [location]`
- `manufacturer / distributor of [product] in Nigeria`

Create category hubs, product pages, supplier pages, state pages, city/LGA pages only where supplier coverage and unique information justify them. Never create thousands of interchangeable SEO pages.

## On-page requirements
- One descriptive H1 aligned with the query.
- Unique title (roughly 50–60 characters where practical) and compelling meta description.
- Short, readable URL.
- Intro explaining buyer problem, local availability and how RNX's intermediary service works.
- Genuine listings with seller name, location, product/service details, last-updated date and verification status.
- Buyer FAQ based on real questions, not keyword stuffing.
- Breadcrumbs and contextual internal links.
- Compressed WebP/AVIF images with descriptive alt text.
- Mobile-first layout, accessible forms, fast LCP/INP/CLS.
- Canonical URL, index/noindex rules, XML sitemap and robots.
- Product, Offer, Service, Organization and Breadcrumb structured data only when page content supports it. Do not mark up fake reviews, prices, stock or seller credentials.

## Lead funnel
Search/social/referral → relevant category/location page → listing or buying guide → call/WhatsApp/request form → qualification → anonymised supplier brief → supplier agrees fee and pays → customer approves introduction → contact details released → outcome recorded.

Capture only information necessary to fulfil the request. Include clear privacy notice and separate marketing opt-in. No scraping or selling personal data. Customer consent must specifically cover the supplier introduction.

## Acquisition channels
1. Google organic search and Search Console.
2. Google Business Profile only for a genuine eligible RNX operation; do not create fake state offices.
3. Helpful buying guides and comparisons.
4. Supplier co-marketing and backlinks from real partners.
5. WhatsApp click-to-chat and opt-in broadcast lists.
6. Facebook/Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn content by vertical.
7. Email alerts for saved searches and new matching inventory (opt-in).
8. Referral links for customers and suppliers.
9. Industry associations, cooperatives, trade groups and events.
10. Repeat purchase reminders and quote follow-ups.

## KPIs
Track sessions by landing page/state, organic impressions/clicks, search query, CTA clicks, calls, WhatsApp clicks, form completion, qualified lead rate, supplier acceptance, paid lead rate, average fee, refunds/credits, close rate, repeat buyers and supplier retention.

## Launch sequence
- Begin with Kano and 1–2 evidence-backed categories.
- Manually verify 5–10 suppliers per category.
- Secure written lead-fee acceptance before selling customer introductions.
- Publish only pages supported by local supplier coverage and unique buyer information.
- Expand into next states when the first market produces qualified enquiries and paid introductions.
