# RNX SEO and marketing playbook

## Search architecture
Build pages around distinct intent: product, service, supplier, quote, repair, wholesale, rental, spare part, procurement and location. Use useful category content and internal links to guide visitors to requests.

## Indexing rules
- Do not index mass-generated state/category pages without unique information.
- Add local landing pages only when they contain researched local context, real supplier coverage or useful sourcing information.
- Publish product/service schema only for real visible listings with accurate details.
- Use LocalBusiness schema only with accurate business details and permission.
- Avoid doorway pages, fabricated stock, fake reviews, invented prices and unsupported supplier counts.

## Technical SEO
- Keep canonical URLs on `https://ruffneck-exchange.vercel.app` until the production domain changes.
- Maintain descriptive titles, unique descriptions, clean URLs, breadcrumbs, internal links, image alt text, robots and sitemap.
- Validate mobile usability and performance.
- Connect Search Console and submit the sitemap after deployment verification.

## Acquisition channels
Use useful buying guides, comparison articles, supplier partnerships, opt-in referrals, social distribution, opt-in email/WhatsApp and saved-search alerts. Obtain consent before marketing messages.

## Measurement
Track search impressions/clicks, form starts/completions, qualified requests, anonymised supplier offers, paid introductions, disputes and supplier close rates. Do not publish keyword volume, conversion or supplier availability claims without measurement.
