Supplier catalogue export
- Sign in to the supplier portal and open Your products and services.
- Under Export your catalogue, choose all listings or a moderation-status group.
- Select Download catalogue CSV.
- The download is a point-in-time snapshot for reporting and backup; it is not an import template. The export does not change listings.
- The API exports only listings belonging to the authenticated supplier. It excludes customer contact details and private image-storage paths.
- A single export can contain up to 10,000 listings. Export by status group if the catalogue is larger.
