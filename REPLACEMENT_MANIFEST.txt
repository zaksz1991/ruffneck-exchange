NEW IN v3.2.0 — SUPPLIER COMMERCIAL QUOTATIONS
- Approved suppliers can submit a priced commercial proposal only after the matching introduction passes supplier acceptance, payment verification, customer approval and contact release.
- Quotes contain title, scope, total amount, three-letter currency, delivery/payment terms, optional fulfilment estimate and 1–60 day validity.
- Customers can review submitted proposals in their account dashboard, mark one proposal as preferred or decline it.
- Only one proposal can be preferred per sourcing request; previous selections and competing open proposals are retained as not-selected history.
- Suppliers can withdraw an unselected quote and issue a revised proposal after decline, withdrawal or expiry.
- Quote expiry is applied on dashboard reads and customer response attempts. Private quote event history and an in-app customer notification are recorded; existing Resend configuration is reused for supplier preference email alerts.
- The commercial quote is separate from the RNX introduction fee. Customer selection is a preference only, not payment, order, binding contract or RNX guarantee.
