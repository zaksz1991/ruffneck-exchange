NEW IN v3.1.0 — VERIFIED TRANSACTION OUTCOMES AND SUPPLIER REVIEWS
- After an introduction has verified payment, customer approval and contact release, the customer and matched supplier can independently confirm its transaction outcome.
- Matching completion confirmations mark the introduction completed. Matching non-completion confirmations mark it cancelled. Conflicting confirmations mark it disputed and exclude it from verified review eligibility.
- An account-linked customer can submit one 1–5 star review after dual confirmation of completion.
- Reviews remain private until an authorised RNX trust staff member approves publication.
- Suppliers may publish one response to an approved review.
- Public supplier profiles show approved reviews and the average rating calculated over the latest up to 50 displayed approved reviews.
