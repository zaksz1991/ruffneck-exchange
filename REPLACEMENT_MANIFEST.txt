NEW IN v3.3.0 — COMMERCIAL QUOTATION OVERSIGHT
- Adds a private Operations console at /admin/quotes to monitor the latest 250 commercial quotations.
- Displays quote status, supplier business name, request category/location, proposal amount and terms, validity and recorded quote event history.
- Supports status filtering, text search and CSV export of the visible records.
- The page and API require the existing operations:read permission (owner and operations_admin roles). Customer names and contact fields are not selected or returned.
- The console is read-only and explicitly distinguishes supplier proposals from RNX revenue, payments, orders, contracts and fulfilment.
