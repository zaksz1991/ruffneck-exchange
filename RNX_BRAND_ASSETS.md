# RNX Brand Assets — v1.4.0

## Included
- `public/brand/rnx-logo.svg` — primary dark-on-light wordmark.
- `public/brand/rnx-logo-light.svg` — light-background wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `public/brand/rnx-symbol.svg` and `public/icon.svg` — compact brand symbol/favicon.
- `public/brand/rnx-marketplace-banner.svg` — website hero/promo banner and social preview.
- `public/marketing/rnx-introduction-flyer.svg` — editable vector flyer for print and digital distribution.
- `public/placeholders/*.svg` — category placeholder illustrations. These are illustrations, not photographs or representations of real stock.

## Brand direction
Charcoal `#101512`, signal green `#b8f36b`, off-white `#f4f7f2`, deep green `#263b2c`.

## Use
All SVG files are scalable and editable in vector design software. For print, open the flyer SVG in a vector editor and export to PDF at the required paper size. Do not stretch the flyer into a banner; use the dedicated wide banner. Replace category placeholders with genuine supplier-provided product photographs only after permission and moderation.

## Production note
The logo and banner are integrated into the site header, homepage and Open Graph/Twitter metadata. The flyer and category illustrations are included as ready-to-use assets but are not falsely presented as real supplier inventory.
