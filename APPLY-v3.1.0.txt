HOW VERIFIED REVIEWS WORK
- A supplier introduction must have a verified paid status, recorded customer approval and recorded contact release.
- The customer and supplier independently confirm whether the transaction was completed.
- Only matching completion confirmations make the introduction review-eligible.
- Conflicting confirmations are flagged as disputed. No verified review can be submitted for a disputed or cancelled transaction.
- Customers submit reviews from their account dashboard. Guest-only requests are not review-eligible in this release.
- Reviews are private until an authorised RNX trust staff member approves publication in Operations → Verified reviews.
- Suppliers can respond once to a published review.

No new Vercel environment variables or external services are required.
