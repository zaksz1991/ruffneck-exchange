# Initial market research — evidence-led opportunity map

Research snapshot: 15 November 2026. This is a screening map, not proof of monthly search volume, margins or low competition. Publicly available sources show sector activity and competitors; Google Keyword Planner and actual supplier interviews are still required to validate query volumes and willingness to pay.

## National evidence
- Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission (NIPC) identifies crop production, agro-processing, aquaculture/fisheries, livestock, agri-tech, cold chain/logistics and irrigation infrastructure as investment subsectors. It highlights processing value addition, cold-chain gaps and irrigation opportunities. Source: https://nipc.gov.ng/sectors/agriculture
- The Special Agro-Industrial Processing Zones first phase covers Cross River, Imo, Kaduna, Kano, Kwara, Ogun, Oyo and FCT. This is a public programme signal, not a guarantee of private purchasing demand. Source: https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/nigeria-nigeria-special-agro-industrial-processing-zones-program-p-ng-aaa-002
- Propcom+ describes solar-powered agricultural mechanisation (threshers, dryers, irrigation pumps) pilots in Kaduna, Kano, Plateau, Bauchi, Adamawa and Gombe. Source: https://propcomplus.org/document/pilotviability/
- NEPZA's operational-zone listing identifies industrial/logistics/manufacturing activity in several relevant states, including Delta and Kaduna. Source: https://nepza.gov.ng/free-zones/operational-zones/

## Competitor reality
Existing Nigerian multi-category B2B and business directories include B2B.ng, Spronet, DBMartNG and i360.ng. They validate that the category is not competition-free. RNX must differentiate through local, intent-specific customer requests, manual qualification, supplier matching, transparent paid introductions and service coverage—not another generic directory.

## How to select the first niche in each state
Score every niche 1–5 against:
1. Commercial search intent (quote, buy, supplier, repair, wholesale, price).
2. Search demand (Keyword Planner / Trends; no guessed figures).
3. Competitor strength in the exact location and query.
4. Average order value and gross margin potential (supplier interviews).
5. Supplier availability and responsiveness.
6. Lead fee acceptance (ask suppliers to pay for a pilot lead).
7. Repeat purchase / maintenance potential.
8. Cost and difficulty of customer qualification.

Do not launch a state/category page until there is at least: distinct local content, a verified serviceable supplier or clear RFQ handling process, useful buyer information, and a working enquiry route. Avoid indexing thin doorway pages.
