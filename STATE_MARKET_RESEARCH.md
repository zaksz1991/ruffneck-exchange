# State and niche market research framework

This document is a research framework, not a claim that demand, search volume, supplier density or profitability has already been measured.

## Candidate sectors to validate
- Agricultural inputs, equipment, processing and packaging.
- Industrial machinery, maintenance and safety supplies.
- Construction materials and specialist installation.
- Power, electrical equipment and renewable energy.
- Refrigeration, HVAC and cold-chain equipment.
- Logistics, warehousing and freight.
- Healthcare equipment and non-prescription medical supplies.
- Business software, IT equipment and digital services.
- Wholesale distribution and procurement.
- Professional and outsourced business services.
- Foodservice, hospitality and commercial supplies.
- Vehicles, fleet services and spare parts.
- Education, workforce training and learning products.
- Retail and consumer goods.

## Validation method by state and city
For each candidate niche, collect:
1. Search demand from Google Keyword Planner and Search Console (where available).
2. Local supplier counts from direct outreach and public business records.
3. Evidence of recurring purchase or repair needs.
4. Typical order value and repeat-purchase frequency from interviews, not invented estimates.
5. Supplier willingness to pay for qualified enquiries.
6. Competition, logistics constraints, seasonality and regulatory requirements.
7. Customer acquisition cost and lead acceptance rates during a small pilot.

Rank opportunities only after evidence is collected. Low apparent competition can indicate low demand, poor economics or difficult supply—not necessarily an attractive niche.
