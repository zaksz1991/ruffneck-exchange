PRIVACY / METRIC INTERPRETATION
Listing detail views are recorded only after the visitor has accepted optional analytics. The browser deduplicates each listing once per tab session. Views are not unique-person counts and exclude visitors who decline analytics. Saves and enquiries are aggregate totals; neither metric proves a completed sale.

No new Vercel environment variables or external services are required.
